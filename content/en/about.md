## Why cold email

Of everything we have run for clients, three things reliably bring business: a website that is found, search positioning, and cold email. Cold email is the fastest of the three. A well-built campaign gets its first replies in days, and tells you within a month whether a market wants what you sell.

## How we work

One person is accountable for your campaign from the first call to the monthly report. Lists are short and verified, emails are written by hand and validated by you, and nothing is sent from your main domain. We read every reply and send you the positive ones the same day; you answer them yourself, because a prospect wants to hear from you, not from an agency.

## Where we work

From France, for clients in France and the United Kingdom. Campaigns run in French or English, sometimes both, and every page of this site exists in both languages.

## The tools we use

Instantly for sending and warm-up, Reoon for verification, Outscraper for sourcing, Spaceship and Namecheap for domains, Calendly for booking. We own nothing in those companies; we use them because they work.
