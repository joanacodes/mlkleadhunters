## The situation

Eden Corporate Mobility houses employees on assignment: furnished apartments and relocation support for companies that move staff for weeks or months at a time. Listings and advertising reach individuals; the real buyers are HR teams, office managers and relocation agencies, who book housing for others and rarely search for it themselves.

## What we did

We targeted companies of a size that relocates people regularly, and the roles inside them that handle it. The sequence offered one thing: a reliable, invoiced alternative to hotels and short-term platforms, with a single contact and none of the deposit headaches. Emails were plain, three per sequence, and every positive reply was forwarded to Eden the same day.

## What came back

The replies came from the right desks. Meetings were with HR and office managers who had a concrete need in the coming months, which is exactly the conversation Eden wanted to have.
