## What we do

We interview you for an hour about the offer, the objections you hear and the words your clients actually use. Then we write a sequence of three emails: a first one that names a specific situation and asks a simple question, and two follow-ups that add one reason each.

The emails are plain text, under a hundred and twenty words, with no images, no links in the first message, and one call to action: a reply. They read like something a real person typed, because that is what gets answers.

## What you get

- A three-email sequence, in your language and your tone, with two variants of the first email.
- A written rationale: who it targets, what it promises, why it is worded that way.
- Rewrites between campaigns based on the replies we receive.

## What we never write

Mass-mailing language, fake personalisation, invented urgency, or anything comparing you to a named competitor. The emails represent you; they have to be something you would say across a table.
