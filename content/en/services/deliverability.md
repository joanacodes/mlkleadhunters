## What we do

We register two or three domains close to yours, set up the DNS records that prove the emails are legitimate (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), create the mailboxes and connect them to the sending tool. Then we warm them: automated, natural-looking exchanges that teach inbox providers to trust the new addresses.

Only after that do campaigns start, at a volume each mailbox can carry. We rotate sending across mailboxes, keep bounces low, and watch the numbers that predict trouble before it arrives.

## What you get

- Two to three dedicated domains and the mailboxes that go with them, owned by you.
- Authentication in place and tested: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, custom tracking domain.
- Warm-up completed and documented, with a placement test before the first campaign.
- Weekly monitoring while campaigns run, and a plan if anything degrades.
